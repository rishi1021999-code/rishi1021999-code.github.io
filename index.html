Margin Walk Studio

Margin Walk Studio is a private tool used by the owner of the YouTube channel Margin Walk to produce and manage that channel's own videos.

What it does

  • Uploads the channel's own finished videos to YouTube as private (optionally with a scheduled release time), and adds titles, descriptions, captions, thumbnails and playlists.
  • Reads the channel's own video list and status to check that uploads match what was intended.
  • Reads the channel's own YouTube Analytics reports (read-only) to see which videos perform well.

It is used only by the channel owner, on the owner's own computer. It is not offered to the public and has no sign-up.
